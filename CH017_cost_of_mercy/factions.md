## Faction Notes

- `FAC_CH017_TRUNG_FAMILY` is the emotional core. Every cost hits this family first: Binh's bowl, Mai's counting, Trung's guilt, Hoang's accusation.
- `FAC_CH017_BASE_COMMUNITY` is not unified. Workers resent refugees, Doctor is torn between lab and wounded, Ong Tu Nieu faces an empty pot.
- `FAC_CH017_REFUGEES` must not be anonymous. Ba Sau is fierce leadership. So 4 is Binh's mirror. The injured father is a ticking clock.
- `FAC_CH017_HOANG_PRAGMATISTS` grows organically from accurate scarcity. Hoang is not a villain; he is a correct accountant of losses.
- `FAC_CH017_WORKER_FACTION` resentment is structural, not moral. They do the hardest work on the least food.
- `FAC_CH017_BANDIT_NETWORK` operates through markers and noise. The spotter does not fight; the marker does the talking.
- `FAC_CH017_CULT` and `FAC_CH017_EDEN_NETWORK` are watching, not attacking. Their threat is informational: every rescue marks the base as having supply, mercy to exploit, and possibly hiding Binh.
