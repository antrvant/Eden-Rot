## Enemy Handling Notes

- Canal infected are standard infected encounters; crawling from water under bridge creates vertical threat during rescue.
- Rushed horde is the main action setpiece enemy; large numbers force bridge retreat and fuel expenditure.
- Bandit spotter is never confronted directly in combat. The threat is the noise lure and intelligence gathering. Do not create a chase/combat encounter; the spotter escapes.
- Hunger and resentment are systemic enemies, not killable targets. They manifest through dialogue tension, rationing scenes, and theft.
- Supply theft is an investigation enemy; resolution comes in Chapter 18.
- Bandit network operates through markers and surveillance; direct confrontation is for later chapters.
