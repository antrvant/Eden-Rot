## Canon Summary

Base "Nha" receives a weak radio distress signal: a refugee convoy is trapped at a collapsed canal bridge with children, wounded, and infected nearby. The council splits over whether to rescue or conserve supplies. Hoang argues for recon-only; Mai insists children are dying; Doctor warns lab supplies will be lost; Worker Lead fears fence collapse without labor. Trung proposes a limited rescue team: children and movable wounded first, supply recovery second, no promises to bring everyone home. The vote passes with Hoang dissenting. At the bridge, the team finds the convoy is real but marked by bandits. Ba Sau leads the refugees with fierce pragmatism. Doctor triages an injured father. Mai discovers a child under the bus who only answers to "So 4" (Number 4), having been numbered for convoy headcounts. The team must choose medicine or fuel from a trapped vehicle while a bandit spotter draws infected with gunfire. Hoang saves lives but loses fuel. At the bridge retreat, Trung overrides the plan to save more children; Hoang holds the bridge longer than he wants, saves the last group, but spends the final fuel can as a fire wall. Back at base, Ong Tu Nieu serves thinner porridge. Binh notices and offers half his bowl to So 4. Mai tells him he can share but does not owe the adults' mercy. Worker Lead complains about reduced labor rations. Doctor reports lab delayed two days. Hoang delivers the core line: "Save everyone, then tonight give your child mercy to drink." That night, supply is stolen from inside the base. Trung finds a bandit checkpoint marker. Hoang privately copies the checkpoint location from the map, setting up Chapter 18.
