## Enemy Handling Notes

- The school infected are a standard clear encounter; no boss mechanics.
- The brick yard pack is the only active combat encounter; four infected are manageable but test group coordination.
- The yellow jacket scout is never seen directly. The threat is the package, not a fight. Do not create a combat encounter with the scout.
- Hunger and fear are systemic enemies, not killable targets. They manifest through dialogue tension, resource meters, and governance dilemmas.
- Contaminated water is resolved through the SQ_Base_01 water purification chain; failure creates a Chapter 15 disease event.
