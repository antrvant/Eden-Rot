## Canon Summary

After rescuing Binh and the children from the factory, Trung's group can no longer keep running. Exhausted, with wounded workers, sick children, and Ong Tu Nieu dragging an injured leg, they find an abandoned elementary school and decide to make it their first base. The player must clear the area, assign roles, ensure water/food/shelter, build a fence, and draft the first governance rules for survivors. The chapter is structured around base-building as an emotional and moral decision, not just a gameplay system. Mai defines "home" by whether children can sleep and be called by name. Trung must learn to lead a community, not just protect his family. Hoang accepts the perimeter role but warns that opening the base too wide will kill everyone. Ong Tu Nieu seeks redemption through cooking under supervision. Binh begins drawing observations that help the base but must be protected from being exploited as a resource. On the first night, a package appears outside the gate: food, medicine, and a letter offering 30 days of safety in exchange for handing over "B-07 / EDENROT CLASS: SILENT BLOOD / EDEN NODE." Trung rejects the trade, pins the letter as evidence, and Mai writes on the blackboard: "Here, children have names." Binh writes his own name below it.
