## Faction Notes

- `FAC_CH014_SURVIVOR_COMMUNITY` is not a unified group. It contains workers who distrust Ong Tu Nieu, wounded who need care, and silent villagers who may or may not be admitted.
- `FAC_CH014_HOANG_PRAGMATISTS` is not a formal faction. It represents the survival instinct that says "cut the weakest to save the strong." Hoang embodies this but is not a villain.
- `FAC_CH014_EDEN_NETWORK` operates through proxies and language. The yellow jacket scout is a courier, not a fighter. The real weapon is the offer letter.
- `FAC_CH014_ONG_TU_NIEU_REDEMPTION` is conditional. His trust can grow or collapse based on future chapters.
- The base's moral identity emerges from how these factions interact: who gets in, who cooks, who guards, and whether a child can be traded for safety.
