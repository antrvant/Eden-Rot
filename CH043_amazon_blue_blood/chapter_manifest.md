## Canon Summary

Rebirth enters the flooded Amazon to reach Aster Node and the Orison Archive. The team must navigate living waterways, avoid Architect-altered ecology, survive Stalker Pack pursuit, protect child-resonance data ethically, and endure a forced split that sends each group toward a different face of the Architect Citadel. Trung and Mai's promise "no one saves the world alone" is immediately tested when Amazon forces each person to survive on a separate branch. The Amazon is revealed as EDENROT CLASS: PREDATORY MEMORY BASIN - a living system the Architect turned into predatory memory that records traces of love to use as traps. The Stalker Pack learns player habits across encounters, targeting bonds, routes, names, and supplies instead of only bodies. One group reaches a clearing where a tree the size of a tower opens like a wound, revealing metal, vines, light, and a gate pulsing with Aster Node. Architect voice: "Welcome to the part of Earth that stopped asking permission."
