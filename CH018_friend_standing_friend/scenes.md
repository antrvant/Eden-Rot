## Scene Notes

- Scene 1 establishes paranoia without resolving it. Evidence points to external bandit markers, but the base does not know that yet.
- Scene 2 is Hoang's solo POV. It must feel like a man convincing himself, not a villain monologue. His repeated self-justifications should feel human and desperate.
- Scene 3 is the betrayal act. The bandit trader should be charming and horrifying through businesslike language. The EDEN broker appears briefly, creating long-term intel weight.
- Scene 4 is investigation and discovery. Trung finds clues slowly, driven by denial breaking. The checkpoint seal on fuel cans is the physical evidence that breaks Hoang's lie.
- Scene 5 is the emotional confrontation. Hoang's "I sold coordinates, not a soul" and "A soul is worth less than gas" are the chapter's core lines. Binh appearing at the door stops Trung from violence.
- Scene 6 is the consequence. Bandits use the sold guard gap to enter. Mai evacuates children, hides Binh, and is captured while saving others. She leaves chalk marks. Hoang fights to prove he did not want this outcome.
- Scene 7 is the fallout. Binh's "Did Uncle Hoang sell Mom?" is the devastating question. The chapter ends with urgency, not resolution: Mai must be rescued before friendship can be judged.
