## Canon Summary

After supply theft and resource crisis, Hoang secretly trades route and security information with a bandit checkpoint for fuel and medicine. He believes he sold nothing personal, but bandits infer the base has children, a classroom, a lab needing fuel, and rumors of an Edenrot-immune child. Trung investigates the theft, follows clues to Hoang's absence, and confronts him. Before council can resolve the matter, bandits use the sold information to raid the school back gate, targeting the classroom for hostages. Mai evacuates children but is captured while saving Lan, Phuc, and So 4. She leaves chalk marks as a trail. Binh asks Hoang directly if he sold his mother. The chapter ends with Trung preparing a rescue mission to the bandit fort, unable to trust Hoang behind him but needing every fighter.
