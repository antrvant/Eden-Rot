## Enemy Notes

- Bandits in Chapter 018 are not random raiders. They are organized predators of scarcity who understand fuel economy, child leverage, and information trade.
- ENM_CH018_BANDIT_LIEUTENANT is not the final boss. The bandit fort leader (likely Lord Duc or a subordinate) is Chapter 19 territory.
- The raid squad uses Hoang's sold guard gap information precisely. They do not assault the front; they use smoke and noise to pull guards away from the classroom, then extract through the back.
- ENM_CH018_BANDIT_CHECKPOINT_GUARDS are not directly fought in this chapter unless the player chooses to attack or follow after the trade. The recommended canon path is observation or arrival-after.
- The bandit trader (npc_bandit_trader) is listed in characters.md as an NPC, not an enemy. He operates through language and leverage, not direct violence in this chapter.
