## Character Notes

- Hoang must believe three lies: he can control what the buyer does with information; information is not personal without a name; saving the base's body justifies cutting its soul. His tragedy is that all three contain enough truth to survive in his head.
- Trung misses signs because he trusts Hoang and because part of him understands the temptation. His wound: not only "Hoang betrayed me" but "I knew the shape of his fear and still let it walk out."
- Mai must not become passive during capture. She moves children, leaves chalk marks, protects Binh's location, misleads bandits about which child matters, and chooses to be taken if it keeps children safe.
- Binh's worldview takes a hit. He has just learned consent and names; now he learns someone he trusted can say "I was trying to help" after causing harm.
- Bandits are not random raiders. They are predators of scarcity who understand fuel, children, information, and good people's predictable choices as leverage.
