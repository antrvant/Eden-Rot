## Faction Notes

- `FAC_CH018_BASE_HOME` should not collapse entirely because of established rules: quarantine, child room, council, guard duty. These reduce damage even when security fails.
- `FAC_CH018_BANDIT_NETWORK` is the first organized antagonist faction to have a real presence. They understand markets: fuel, children, information, and predictable choices of good people.
- `FAC_CH018_EDEN_NETWORK` is only hinted. The broker's full identity and connection to the Architect/EDENROT system is a long-term reveal.
- `FAC_CH018_HOANG_FRACTURE` is not a gameplay faction but an internal trust state. It tracks the damage to the Trung-Hoang friendship and its ripple effects on Binh and the base.
- `FAC_CH018_BASE_CHILDREN` gains importance because the classroom is the specific raid target. The child-room rules from earlier chapters prove their value here.
