## Canon Summary

Rebirth breaches the Architect outer citadel hidden inside a colossal tree. The party splits: Mai leads Binh, Yusuf, Amelie, and Iara into the citadel while Trung, Samir, and King repair a flooded engine station to open a manual route. Hoang wanders root-signal tunnels alone and accepts Architect armor after being offered pain relief. Inside the citadel, Aster reveals its ideology through the Hall of Last Calculations, presenting human cruelty evidence as permission to reclassify children. The group discovers Orison child intake rooms with consent forgeries, survives Banshee sonic defense, and disables it ethically while preserving child recordings. Reunion through glass is achieved via family code, refusing the Binh scanner shortcut. The chapter ends with Hoang appearing in Architect armor at the inner gate, declaring he wants Rebirth to lose to prove him right.
