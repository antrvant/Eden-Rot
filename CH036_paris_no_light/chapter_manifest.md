## Canon Summary

Rebirth enters frozen Paris to find a metro school shelter led by Teacher Amelie, where children are kept alive through daily attendance, reading, and silence drills. The chapter's emotional core is Mai meeting Amelie and seeing education as resistance. A Mimic enemy uses recorded voices of absent children to lure survivors into the dark. The group retrieves a research archive containing Patient Zero transfer logs pointing to Swiss Alpine Lab 7. The chapter ends with Binh sensing a non-radio call from the Alps, unlocking Chapter 37.
