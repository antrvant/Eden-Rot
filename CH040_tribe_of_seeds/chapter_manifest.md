## Canon Summary

Rebirth enters the seed village through a living bridge to request alliance and proper enzyme. They undergo a listening circle where they hear testimony from raider victims, honestly present and surrender their toxic anti-growth compound, learn seed knowledge connected to cure formula, defend the village from raider retaliation without using poison, harvest proper enzyme under Nia's guidance, and ratify a two-way seed covenant alliance. The chapter ends with Cairo reporting incoming siege pressure and the group departing with enzyme and alliance.
