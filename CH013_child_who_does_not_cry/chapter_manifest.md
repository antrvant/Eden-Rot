## Canon Summary

Trung and Mai track Binh to an industrial zone operating as a child trafficking operation disguised as a production facility. Mai leads the approach by recognizing Binh's marks. The group infiltrates through a drainage ditch, meets Ong Tu Nieu (Old Claypot), an ambivalent old cook who hides rice for children and reveals Binh is "the child who does not cry." The group crosses the forced-labor packing floor where Binh's drawings map guard patrols, discovers a child ledger classifying children by Edenrot Response (fever, rot-sensitive, silent blood), and reaches the children's detention room. In the temporary medical room, Trung and Mai find Binh sitting silent, asking "Are you really my father?" Binh was scratched by infected but did not fever, marking him as immune or dormant. Factory Boss confronts the group with rationalizing language about saving humanity, offering to trade other children for Binh. Ong Tu Nieu triggers a gas distraction. The group escapes through cold storage where a Spitter-mutation foreshadow sprays acid. At the loading yard, Trung and Mai choose to rescue locked children at the cost of delay. Hoang carries Binh to safety. Outside the fence, Binh places two fingers on Trung's hand. Ong Tu Nieu delivers a torn page: "EDENROT CLASS: SILENT BLOOD / EDEN NODE - SILENT SAMPLE." The family is together but changed; Binh's silence is the wound that will take many chapters to heal.
