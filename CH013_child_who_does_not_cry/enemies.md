## Spitter Handling Note

Do not make CH013 a Spitter boss fight. The Spitter-infected appears as a foreshadow in cold storage only. Its acid spit corrodes metal, blocks paths, and burns fabric (Hoang loses his jacket). Canonical outcome is escape, not kill. The Spitter mutation should escalate in later chapters.

## Factory Boss Handling Note

Factory Boss is a social_hazard, not a combat enemy. He does not fight directly. His danger comes from language: rationalizing exploitation as humanitarianism. Recommended canon: he survives or his body is not confirmed, allowing faction continuity and connection to larger EDEN/child network infrastructure.
