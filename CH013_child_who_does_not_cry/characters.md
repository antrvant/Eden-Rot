## Character Notes

- Binh's trauma: holds adult gaze longer than normal to check for lies, flinches at unexpected touch, counts numbers when loud noises occur, eats slowly and hides food, does not cry when meeting parents, does not call "ba"/"me" for many days.
- Mai reads Binh's traces (drawings, step patterns, hiding habits) because she lived every small detail of his life. She accepts stopping when her instinct wants to rush.
- Trung wants to use violence to fix his guilt. Must learn to protect through restraint. Does not make easy promises; promises "you will not be alone."
- Hoang protects Binh out of love for Trung, not family obligation. Sees he is outside the family circle. Seed: later susceptible to factions that talk about "real family."
- Ong Tu Nieu is not a hero. He cooked for the people holding children. But if he did not cook, children would have nothing. He hid rice, changed schedules, kept Binh from being beaten.
- Factory Boss uses management language: lists, allocations, assets, priorities. He believes he is more pragmatic than parents. He can weaponize humanitarian language to legitimize breaking families.
