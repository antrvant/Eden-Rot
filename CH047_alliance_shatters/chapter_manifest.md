## Canon Summary

Rebirth evacuates immune children, Hoang pod, and child records from the collapsing Amazon citadel. Allied factions converge and begin demanding custody and data access. Hunter, an Architect Elite sniper bioform, attacks to fracture trust between factions. Trung rescues a faction squad that nearly betrayed Rebirth, proving the alliance holds even when cracked. The player defeats or repels Hunter, preserves Hoang pod through power failure, and receives the Antarctica countdown broadcast. Mai proposes a Guardian Circle Charter that prevents any single faction from owning children. The alliance departs southward, cracked but moving, with a 30-day countdown to Source rewrite.
