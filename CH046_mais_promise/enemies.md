## Enemy Notes

- Glutton is horror through system: labels like "non-viable" and "spent carrier" make the creature terrifying as ideology made flesh.
- Orison's danger is bureaucratic tenderness: "We are not hurting them. We are preparing them."
- The time locks create urgency without making the chapter feel rushed.
- Power drain creates tension between children and Hoang without making them compete morally.
- Feeder tendrils are the lockdown escalation; they target pods and records, not people directly.
