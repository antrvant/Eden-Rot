## Canon Summary

Rebirth enters Orison prison/lab wing to rescue immune children. Mai leads a consent-first rescue protocol built on names and stop words. The team meets Ana, Mateo, Luz, Rafi, and Nina. Orison confronts Mai ideologically, offering a trade of Binh for twenty-seven children, which is rejected. The team defeats Glutton, a biomass recycler that processes failed children into citadel nutrient gel. After Glutton falls, terraforming engine transfer to Antarctica is discovered with a 30-day countdown. Mai counts rescued children by name. Citadel collapse protocol activates, setting Chapter 47.
