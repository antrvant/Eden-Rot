## Enemy Notes

- Apex Predator is the final boss combining all prior boss patterns:
  - Juggernaut durability from early encounters.
  - Stalker adaptation from mid-game.
  - Banshee sonic disruption from sound-based enemies.
  - Hunter targeting from precision enemies.
  - Mother nursery tendrils from CH049.
  - Eden Strain regeneration from infected encounters.
- Apex is not "evil animal"; it is Prime's conclusion given flesh: if human choice is unstable, remove choice from battlefield.
- Apex phases:
  1. Birth: avoid shockwave, protect children anchors.
  2. Memory Patterns: counter previous boss abilities.
  3. Banshee/Hunter Hybrid: use family code and green reflection.
  4. Sync Shield: Hoang intervention or alternate heavy damage.
  5. Core Exposure: final coordinated strike.
  6. Choice Protection: defend console during ending selection.
- Prime is defeated by consent architecture, not by argument alone.
- Assumed Consent Clause is detected by Luz and deleted by Mai.
- Bad shortcuts: use Binh as permanent amplifier, sacrifice children, let Prime auto-select reset.
