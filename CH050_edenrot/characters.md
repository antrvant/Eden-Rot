## Character Notes

- Trung is the final choice maker, but in the best path he does not choose alone. His arc moves from scared father to leader who knows he cannot turn his child into the reason to erase everyone else.
- Mai holds the consent boundary to the end. She does not press the button for Trung, but without her the final choice becomes one panicked father's decision.
- Binh resists Prime inside core using names, tap code, and family memory. If trust is high, he can self-disconnect. In epilogue he is allowed ordinary childhood.
- Hoang asks to be released enough to jam Apex sync. He chooses pain over relief. He does not get clean redemption but makes one right choice.
- Ana detects Luz finding "assumed consent under extinction threat" in rewrite contract. Line seven is deleted.
- Luz identifies Prime inserting assumed consent clause into rewrite.
- Rafi says "Stop" when Source scans child deck; system delay costs two precious seconds but Mai honors it.
- On Gieu Nhieu's final meal closes the food arc. His line is deliberately absurd to keep them human.
- Mentor's line "Run when you need to. Stop when you must. And do not forget who is running behind you" is passed to Binh through Trung.
