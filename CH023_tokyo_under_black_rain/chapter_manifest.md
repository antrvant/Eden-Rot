## Canon Summary

Nha Di follows the Tokyo relay warning into black rain. The group encounters a Japanese enclave in an old high-speed rail station controlling access to naval codes needed to reach and operate a warship. Mai leads negotiations while Trung restrains his urgency and Hoang is kept under technical supervision. Mai refuses to use Binh as proof or payment, earning Aiko's conditional respect through the shared rule "Here, children have names." To earn trust, the group helps access and disable a relay, triggering Architect Commander Aster's first direct appearance. Aster does not threaten like a villain; it classifies, corrects, and optimizes, calling Binh an "unregistered correction" and recording his name without understanding why names matter. The group obtains naval codes and coordinates to a fallen naval base, with Aiko's warning that automated ships do what frightened humans told them to do before they died.
