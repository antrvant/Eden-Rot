## Canon Summary

Doctor proposes testing Binh's blood to understand why the child did not fever after Eden Strain exposure. Trung refuses immediately; Mai asks for protocol before any sample. Council votes to prepare lab without blood draw. Trung, Hoang, Doctor, and Worker Lead scavenge an abandoned medical station for microscope and supplies, finding Cult symbols on the wall. Mai and Trung explain the test to Binh using chalk drawings; Binh asks if he is still normal. Council drafts Cure Ethics: Binh is a child first, consent can be withdrawn, no enemy labels in records. During blood draw, Binh panics and says stop; everyone stops. After a pause, Binh chooses again voluntarily. Doctor discovers viral dormancy: Binh's blood makes Eden Strain silent, not dead. Hoang grasps strategic value; Mai corrects language from "the boy's blood" to "Binh's blood." Council classifies result. That night, radio picks up Cult chant about "the seed that does not fever." Hanh reveals Cult of Evolution followers are heading toward the base. Binh writes "I said yes" on his own blood sample label, reclaiming ownership from B-07.
