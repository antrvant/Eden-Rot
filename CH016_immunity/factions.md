## Faction Notes

- `FAC_CH016_FAMILY_CORE` tension is not about democracy. It is about whether a father can say no for his son when the question is about his son's body. Trung learns that protecting Binh means giving him the right to say no himself.
- `FAC_CH016_SECURITY` (Hoang) should be written with reason but lacking softness. He is not evil; he is pragmatic. His dissent about delays is recorded but not punished. The danger is that strategic thinking around Binh can make the child feel like a resource.
- `FAC_CH016_MEDICAL` (Doctor) is the character most afraid of doing this wrong. He is not a mad scientist; he is an ethical person in an unethical world. His fear of becoming a softer copy of Factory Boss drives his insistence on rules.
- `FAC_CH016_CULT` should contrast with EDEN: EDEN uses numbers and conditions (B-07, Eden Node); Cult uses faith and language (seed, chosen, bloom). Both are dangerous but in different ways. EDEN wants to classify Binh; Cult wants to worship Binh. Both erase the child.
- `FAC_CH016_CHILDREN` is not a voting faction but must have a voice. Mai's role ensures children are not forgotten or used as political tools. Binh's consent is the chapter's moral test.
- Language factions: Architect language says `Eden Node`; Cult language says `Seed`; ethical survivor language must remain personal: `Binh's blood`, `Binh consented`, `Binh needs protection`.
