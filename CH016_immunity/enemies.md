## Enemy Handling Notes

- Chapter 16 has limited traditional infected combat (clinic scavenge only). The true enemies are ethical, social, and linguistic.
- `ENM_CH016_ETHICS_PRESSURE` is the chapter's central antagonist: the question of whether science can go through a child like an open door.
- `ENM_CH016_CULT_RUMOR_NETWORK` is the cliffhanger threat. Cult members are not seen in person this chapter but are heard via radio and their symbols are found. They should not attack immediately; their language should appear in rumors and symbols.
- `ENM_CH016_INFECTED_NURSE` carries emotional weight because of the nameplate "Hoa." Doctor takes the nameplate as a reminder of whose supplies they are taking.
- `ENM_CH016_INTERNAL_FEAR` is Binh's arc: if the virus sleeps in me, am I still me? Am I part of Edenrot? This fear is addressed through honest conversation, not reassurance.
- `ENM_CH016_STRATEGIC_PRESSURE` through Hoang is not villainy. He sees closing windows and strategic value. The danger is that his language makes Binh feel like a resource.
- `ENM_CH016_LANGUAGE_ROT` is the most insidious enemy. Once survivors start calling Binh by his code or by possessive language, the base is morally infected before anyone is bitten. Cure Ethics exists to fight this.
