## Canon Summary

Rebirth Alliance reaches a Walled Civic Zone seeking supplies and route forward. Mai leads diplomacy at the gate, refusing unsafe biometric scans for Binh. The group investigates refugee conditions outside the wall, discovering numbered refugees, fake entry band traders, and children labeled "low skill household." Mai negotiates with Councilor Ada Price, who demands AI core access, a non-invasive Binh scan, and Hoang's detention. Hoang nearly offers himself in exchange for refugee medical access. A trader militia incites a riot at the south market; Mai stops it without lethal force. Ada accepts Rebirth as allied delegation with a public hearing for Hoang. VALE core displays a Neo-Military convoy approaching the enclave, opening CH029.
