## Canon Summary

The morning after the first night defense, Trung still hears the Screamer in his head and wants to chase the lost radio fragment of Mai. Mentor blocks him until he completes basic training. Trung learns to breathe before firing, avoid friendly targets, move silently through abandoned homes, and prioritize threats instead of shooting the closest danger. The training becomes real during a bus-yard ammo run and a locked armory mission. Trung glimpses Mentor's hidden grief through a child's toy, helps Phuc begin recovering from fear, and faces a Tank/armored infected threat that the team cannot win yet. Mentor orders retreat, and Trung learns that survival does not mean winning every fight. Because he obeys, he earns permission to join the Chapter 009 radio mission.
