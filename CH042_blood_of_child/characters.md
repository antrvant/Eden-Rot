## Character Notes

- Trung's arc is from reflexive ban ("no one touches my child") to structured protection ("set limits and stand beside him"). His hardest moment is sitting still while the needle enters Binh's arm.
- Mai fights language before she fights needles. She corrects "sample" to "Binh's blood", "bridge" to "child", "asset" to "person". Her signature line: "I am not proud that you endured pain. I am proud that you told the truth."
- Binh is both brave and scared. He asks two critical questions: "If I don't give, will Uncle Yusuf die again?" and "Will Mom and Dad stop loving me if I say no?" He chooses the stop word "Den Do" (red light) echoing normal life.
- Samir is good but not immune to the temptation of saving millions. He speaks too fast about "resonance bridge" and freezes the room. He carries Cairo guilt from signing an emergency extraction order.
- Hoang confesses to Trung: "There was a second where I hoped his blood could save me. Then I hated myself for hoping." This deepens friendship through ugly honesty.
- Yusuf rejects debt logic: "Living doesn't mean you owe me the rest of you."
