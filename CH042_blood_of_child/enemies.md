## Enemy Handling Notes

- Chapter 42 has no traditional combat enemies. All threats are social, linguistic, environmental, or informational.
- Time Pressure is the most dangerous antagonist because it is real. The source explicitly states: "Ethics is not hard when we have enough time. Ethics is hard when people die every minute."
- Language Dehumanization must be treated as a game mechanic. Each wrong word (sample, bridge, asset, factor, key, donor, source) should register as a firewall crack. Mai fights this actively.
- Masud's Rumor is partially contained but not eliminated. It remains a threat for future chapters.
- Architect Signal is foreshadow only. Do not turn it into a combat encounter.
- Moving Trees are a Chapter 43 hook. No combat data needed here.
