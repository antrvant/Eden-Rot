## Faction Notes

- FAC_CH042_TRUNG_FAMILY should not behave like a standard reputation faction. It stores emotional trust, hurt, conflict resolution, and family rule state. The family vow "no one saves the world alone" is a long-term flag.
- FAC_CH042_REBIRTH_ALLIANCE is the operational faction but its trust depends on the firewall remaining intact. If firewall cracks, alliance fractures.
- FAC_CH042_MASUD_NETWORK is offscreen-only in this chapter. Its threat is information warfare, not direct combat.
- FAC_CH042_ARCHITECT is a systemic antagonist revealed through data, not presence. The child resonance chamber reframes Act 7 as war against systems that industrialize child resonance.
- FAC_CH042_SABLE_REMNANT is a listening threat only. Resolution deferred to Chapters 43-44.
