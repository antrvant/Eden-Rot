## Canon Summary

Bandit saboteurs open the outpost side gate at night and use false loudspeaker orders to route refugees toward an ambush. A Screamer-led horde attacks. Multiple crisis points erupt simultaneously: radio tent, medical tent, ammo storage, refugee tent. Trung must choose priorities. He confronts a bandit saboteur in a yellow fire jacket who reveals the bandits use "Edenrot" as a fear weapon. Mentor holds the West Gate, is injured, and orders Trung to lead survivors through the drainage tunnel. Mentor gives his dog tag and map. Hoang physically stops Trung from returning. Trung issues his first command to assemble survivors. The group escapes through the drainage tunnel as the ammo storage explodes. Mentor fires his last shots. The outpost burns.
