## Canon Summary

Rebirth prepares and transmits its first global broadcast through NORAD satellite safe mode. In a 4-hour window, the team stabilizes the relay, convenes a broadcast council to decide message content, drafts a promise rather than a command, translates it into multiple languages, strips location metadata to block VALE/Architect tracking, and transmits. The world answers: Paris, Cairo, Amazon, and Atlantic Fleet all respond. A weak ping possibly from Hoang is detected. An Architect terraforming map fragment overrides the screen, revealing that human coordination has accelerated the redesign schedule. The chapter ends with the team choosing the next front.
