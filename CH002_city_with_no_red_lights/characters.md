## Character Notes

- Trung is torn between becoming the kind of person Mai believes he can be and becoming someone who pushes the whole city aside to reach his family.
- Mai is not waiting passively. Every message is a survival action: warning Trung, moving Binh, leaving a fridge note, and marking a trail.
- Hoang must feel like a true friend, not a villain. His danger is that his advice is practical, useful, and emotionally cold.
- Sai Gon functions like a chapter character: familiar city sounds are transformed into alarms, bargaining, lies, and calls for help.
