## Canon Summary

After CH008 training, Trung joins a team (Hoang, Mentor, radio operator, engineer, scout) to restore the old radio station. Along the way they destroy a false safe zone transmitter luring survivors into robbery. At the post office, they scavenge parts while avoiding infected postal workers; Trung collects unsent letters. Trung climbs the rooftop antenna for a timed repair while infected are drawn by generator noise. Mai's signal comes through clearly: she is at a northeast church with a girl, Binh was taken to an industrial zone, and people in yellow coats are hunting Binh specifically. Hoang physically pulls Trung from the microphone when reverse broadcasting risks revealing Mai's position. The signal is jammed by EDEN NODE ACTIVE. The team retreats with Mai's recording. Back at outpost, Trung listens to the recording and agrees to wait one night before acting.
