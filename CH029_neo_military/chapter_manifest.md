## Canon Summary

Army Remnants arrive at the Walled Civic Zone with armored vehicles, fuel trucks, and radar. Colonel Graves demands Trung place Rebirth under military command chain. Trung accepts mission coordination only, not full submission. The group inspects VALE-marked prisoners facing execution under "transport math" policy. At Fort Resolve, Graves shows radar data of Cult of Evolution collecting children. During a desert fuel depot mission, Trung rescues a trapped engineer instead of shelling the depot, and Hoang saves Sergeant Pike from a drone. When Sloane orders execution of marked prisoners due to transport shortage, Trung refuses. A drone/infected ambush forces cooperation, and the marked engineer restarts the radar relay, proving the execution category wrong. Graves grants conditional support: fuel, a radar window, and an escort team. The radar reveals a Cult convoy collecting children toward Red Canyon, opening CH030.
