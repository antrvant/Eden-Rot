## Canon Summary

Chapter 49 descends under Antarctic ice into the Terraforming Source. The team enters through a door that melts like wax without heat. The ice tunnel walls display an archive of human repetition: war, cure hoarding, child exploitation, faction betrayal. Architect Prime speaks for the first time, arguing humanity repeats and reset ends the loop. Trung counters that the evidence is real but the verdict is wrong. Hoang, briefly stabilized by Source interference, warns that relief offered without consent is still a cage. The team reaches the Nursery Threshold, a vast chamber with ecological templates in cradle-like structures. Mother, the nursery guardian, activates to protect the templates. After surviving Mother's phase one, Prime offers to scale cure globally using Binh as a permanent consent bridge. Mai refuses before Binh must answer, and Binh says he wants cure, not cage. Using tribal bio-covenant knowledge, the team redirects Mother's nutrient flows instead of destroying the nursery. Prime then presents the full reset argument: end the repeating loop by ending humanity. Trung responds that ending the patient is not healing. Mother/Source grabs Binh during overload, forcibly connecting him to the core. The stop word triggers. Everyone stops using Binh. If Binh trust is high, he resists Prime internally. Immune children use tap code and names to anchor him. The final choice console opens with four options: Destroy Source, Authorize Reset, Rewrite Source, and Hybrid Rebirth Path (conditional). Chapter 49 arms these options but does not execute them. Binh, connected to core, speaks to Trung: "Dad, do not let me be the reason you forget how to be human." The final battlefield opens for Chapter 50.
