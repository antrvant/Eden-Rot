## Drowned Infected Notes

Drowned infected are not traditional zombies that swim. They do not swim. They wait. The water carries them. Sound and vibration stir them. This is a key ecological distinction from land infected. Doctor says: "Maybe it does not swim, but the water is learning to carry it."

## Bandit Notes

Bandit pursuers are motivated by the Architect drive and potential hostages. They follow the group from base through the river to the port. They are human enemies, not infected.

## Edenrot Notes

Edenrot is survivor slang for Eden Strain-contaminated water zones. Architects call it "Eden Node" or "Maritime Relay." Survivors call it Edenrot: it is beautiful, bioluminescent, and corroding everything. The bio-storm is the first active Edenrot event the group encounters at sea. It cannot be defeated; it must be survived.

## Bio-Storm Handling

Do not make the bio-storm a combat encounter. The canonical outcome is forced movement toward the unnamed island. The group cannot fight the storm, only endure it and try to keep the ship intact.
