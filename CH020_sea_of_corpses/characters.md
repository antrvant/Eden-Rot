- Trung leaves without certainty. Leaving base is leadership, not defeat.
- Mai's chalk/blackboard fragment symbolizes preserving the rule system and emotional culture of "Home."
- Binh's identity milestone: leaving name behind and carrying chalk means identity does not disappear when location changes.
- Hoang should feel uncomfortable in every scene. He is competent, sometimes right, and not safe. This limbo is the point.
- Lam is suspicious but practical. His promise about his brother's ship Mê Linh is his price.
- Thu is hostile to strangers but respects the Architect drive and technical skill.
- Doctor is focused on Binh's blood sample and Eden biology; bio-storm concerns relate to viral dormancy.
- So 4 mirror with Binh creates the goodbye: one stays, one goes, chalk connects them.
