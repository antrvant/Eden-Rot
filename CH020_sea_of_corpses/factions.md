## Faction Notes

- The key language split in this chapter: Architects say "EDEN NODE ACTIVE" and "MARITIME RELAY ONLINE." Survivors say "Edenrot." Both refer to the same corrupted ecosystem.
- Hoang's limbo status is a faction of one: he is needed, not trusted, not forgiven. Every interaction with him should carry tension.
- Lam and Thu are not formally allied with Trung's group; they are transactional allies whose loyalty depends on promises kept and skills respected.
- Ba Sau's convoy-marked cloth is a future thread: if the group meets her convoy contacts at sea, it could open trade or alliance.
