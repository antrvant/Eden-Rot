## Canon Summary

Chapter 20 opens Act 4 by expanding the world from rural base to the Mekong River, coast, and South China Sea. After obtaining the Architect broker drive in Chapter 019, the group must leave their first base. Hoang is judged by council: not forgiven, not killed, but sent along under supervision because his skills are needed to decode the naval route. The family says goodbye to Classroom 3B and the blackboard where children's names are written. Mai takes a chalk fragment as a portable symbol of "Home." The group travels downriver through corpse-filled waterways where drowned infected stir in Edenrot-contaminated zones. They meet river pilot Lam at an abandoned ferry dock, who helps them cross in exchange for a promise about his brother's ship. At the coastal fishing port, mechanic Thu guards an unnamed cargo ship that needs repair. Bandit pursuers attack and drowned infected rise from under the ship. The group escapes on the vessel. At sea, the family processes leaving Vietnam. That night, a bio-storm hits: black rain, glowing corpses, radio picking up "EDEN NODE ACTIVE" and "MARITIME RELAY ONLINE" from an unnamed island. The ship is pushed toward land not on any map.
