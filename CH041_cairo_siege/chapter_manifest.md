## Canon Summary

Chapter 41 transitions from knowledge gathering to the first real experiment. Rebirth escorts Proper Bio-Enzyme into Cairo, reactivates a buried temporary lab beneath a bombed museum, and synthesizes the first cure from Patient Zero data. Three simultaneous pressures converge: external siege from infected, raiders, and an Architect-guided Juggernaut; internal lab pressure as Doctor Samir merges data under power, blood, and time shortages; and family pressure as Trung wants to save the world but not at the cost of his child. Yusuf, a 17-year-old water mechanic, becomes the first cure recipient through informed consent. The cure succeeds on Yusuf but fails across all other batch samples, revealing a missing resonance factor. Binh overhears Samir discussing the resonance bridge requirement and demands honesty about his role. Masud attempts a cure ransom which is refused. The Juggernaut is defeated or trapped through resin, cooling vent, and crane pin tactics. The Architect beacon is recovered pointing to Amazon/Aster Node for Act 7. Cairo lab is compromised; Rebirth evacuates to a mobile lab.
