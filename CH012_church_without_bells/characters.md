## Character Notes

- Trung must not blame Mai for Binh's loss. His line "They took him. That is different" is the emotional anchor of the chapter.
- Mai is strong but not invincible. She is wounded, guilt-ridden, and still saving others. Her power lies in continuing to act while breaking.
- Hoang has a defining moment: stepping back for the couple's privacy, then stepping forward to say they must leave. He bridges emotion and survival.
- Priest is not a saint. He has seen people wearing rescue coats, has locked the church bell to stay alive. His faith has learned silence but not died.
- Girl is proof Mai saved someone while losing her own child. She should be shy, remember small details, and trust Mai more than any adult.
- Yellow coat watcher is a social threat, not a combat encounter. Detection triggers urgency, not a fight.
- O Tu Nieu is seeded as the contact in the industrial zone for CH013.
