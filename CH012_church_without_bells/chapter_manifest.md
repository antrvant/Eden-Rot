## Canon Summary

After the outpost collapses, Trung follows Mai's radio fragment and chalk trail to an old church northeast of the city. The church bell is wrapped in cloth and silenced to avoid attracting zombies. Priest guards the gate with suspicion toward armed strangers. A young girl saved by Mai leads Trung to the prayer room basement where Mai waits. The reunion is joyful but cut by the truth: Binh was separated from Mai and taken by yellow-coated hunters to the industrial zone because the boy did not develop a fever after exposure. Mai has been protecting church survivors and leaving chalk trails while unable to leave without drawing watchers to Binh. Hoang detects a yellow coat observer watching the church. The group must leave immediately for the industrial zone before the hunters return.
