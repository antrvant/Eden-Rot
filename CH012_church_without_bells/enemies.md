## Yellow Coat Handling Note

The yellow coat watcher is a social/perception threat, not a combat encounter. Detection triggers urgency and departure, not a boss fight. The watcher's escape can reduce the group's available time at the church. Do not create a yellow coat combat encounter in this chapter; they are a faction threat seeded for CH013 and beyond.

## Edenrot Handling Note

Edenrot is not an enemy to fight. It is a zone hazard that survivors route around. The environmental danger is atmospheric and lore-building: corpses that do not decompose normally, vegetation growing from bedrooms, death that does not behave as expected. Players avoid it; they do not engage it.
