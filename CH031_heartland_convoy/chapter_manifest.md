## Canon Summary

The morning after Red Canyon, the convoy must process Hoang's absence while integrating rescued children. Binh sees Hoang's empty seat and asks hard questions. Mai insists the betrayal be recorded in a truth archive but not shouted at children during evacuation. The convoy forms as a mobile community with named vehicles, roles, and rules. At a truck stop, road bandits posing as relief workers are exposed through Mai's observation that children are kept away from food. A VALE drone hunter tracks Binh but is confused by Hoang's false mark pings sent from miles away. At night, Mai holds a name circle where rescued children reclaim their identities; Mother Elian apologizes to Noah without asking forgiveness. Hoang sends a coded warning about an ambush ahead. At the grain elevator, Cult scouts and bandits converge; Hoang appears from outside, shoots the drone's wing, saves Binh, then flees across the rail line. Trung chooses to protect the convoy rather than chase Hoang. An emergency broadcast node reveals NORAD coordinates, leading to Chapter 32.
