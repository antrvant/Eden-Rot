## Canon Summary

With warship secured and Nha Di attached as home, the group crosses the Pacific. They formalize weapons doctrine: no autonomous lethal fire on humans, no fire near civilians, council witness required. The family shares one rare calm night: warm porridge, star-gazing, Binh's questions. Hoang sees satellite ping consequence of his coordinate packet but preserves evidence and does not confess immediately. A satellite pulse summons hundreds of sea-infected toward the warship. Trung refuses full autonomous defense, using decoys and limited fire. Swimmers board at hull and Nha Di lines. Aster speaks through static: "Correction vector converging." The group survives but uses fuel and ammo; no clean win. As they near the West Coast, a drone/satellite reflection tracks them. Hoang tries to confess but alarm interrupts. Act 5 begins with EDENROT surveillance overhead and on shore.
