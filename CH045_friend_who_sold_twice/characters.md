## Character Notes

- Hoang is not a puppet. He is a wounded person inside a device that makes surrender feel like healing. He must remain recognizably Hoang throughout, especially when dangerous.
- The emotional truth: love cannot fix everything instantly, but without love the only options left are execution or surrender.
- Trung fights with two opposing instincts: protect Binh/Mai at all cost, and not turn Hoang into a nameless target.
- Mai protects Binh without erasing his voice. She calls Hoang by name, never "target."
- Binh learns he can love someone and still say no. His boundary with Hoang is the chapter's moral core.
- Aster uses Hoang as a living argument. It wants Rebirth to kill Hoang to prove love is conditional, or release him to prove ethics is weak.
