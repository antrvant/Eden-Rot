## Canon Summary

Rebirth confronts Hoang in Architect armor at the citadel inner gate. The team must resist Aster's moral trap, refuse to use Binh as armor stabilizer, survive a non-lethal fight against Hoang, and choose his fate. Aster frames the confrontation as a wager: kill Hoang and prove love is conditional, release him and prove ethics cannot guard children, or capture him and prove Rebirth prefers suffering over clarity. Rebirth names the missing fourth option: relief without ownership. Through memory chamber phases, the group confronts Hoang's past rescue and coordinate betrayal honestly. Binh sets a boundary with the person he loves. The canon path captures Hoang alive using stasis restraint, interrupting the ownership loop without clean forgiveness. While the team is delayed, Orison moves immune children deeper into the prison/lab wing, setting up Chapter 46.
