## Character Notes

- Trung's climax is not killing the lieutenant. It is choosing not to execute Hoang or leave him to die. This does not absolve Hoang. It defines Trung.
- Mai must remain active: she reads guard schedule, misdirects about Binh, leaves chalk, protects other captive, creates opening during rescue. She is not a damsel.
- Hoang helping rescue is not redemption. It is damage control. He should feel the difference and hate it. His future arc remains open: exile, redemption, deeper fall, or final sacrifice.
- Binh's question "Did you kill Uncle Hoang?" should not be treated as childish. It is the moral center. He is asking what justice looks like when the person who hurt you was once family.
- Ba Sau calls Mai "your smart daughter" and Trung corrects her to "my wife." Ba Sau explains that in the community, smart people have become everyone's children. This echoes Mai's role beyond family.
- Worker Lead enforces that Hoang goes with tied hands and under guard. He represents the council's authority over Trung's personal judgment.
- The bandit lieutenant is not cartoonish. He sees hostages as insurance. He has broker connections and trade files.
