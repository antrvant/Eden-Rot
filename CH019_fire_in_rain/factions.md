## Faction Notes

- `FAC_CH019_FAMILY_CORE` is tested in this chapter. Trung and Mai are reunited but the reunion is not clean. Hoang stands apart. The family holds, but it holds damage.
- `FAC_CH019_RESCUE_TEAM` is a forced alliance. Worker Lead's presence ensures Hoang's guidance is used without giving him autonomy. Trung leads but his authority is personal, not council-granted.
- `FAC_CH019_BASE_DEFENSE` proves the base can function without Trung present. Doctor's role shifts from medical to intel analysis, foreshadowing Act 4's maritime investigation.
- `FAC_CH019_BANDITS` are weakened but not destroyed. The lieutenant survives. The faction loses its server and trade capability but retains personnel. They can return.
- `FAC_CH019_CHILDREN` are the moral audience. Binh's question "Did you kill Uncle Hoang?" is the chapter's ethical center. So 4's addition to the blackboard shows children can repair what adults break.
- `FAC_CH019_EDEN_ARCHITECT_NETWORK` is the scale expansion. The server files confirm that the hunt for Binh is not local; it has maritime logistics, broker payments, and Architect-level tracking. This opens Act 4.
