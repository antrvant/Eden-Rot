## Canon Summary

Trung leads a rescue team to the bandit fort in an old brick factory to save Mai, forced to use Hoang's knowledge of bandit routes despite Hoang's recent betrayal. Mai, captive inside, counts guards by their coughs, leaves chalk marks on bricks and floors, misdirects the lieutenant about Binh's location, and comforts another prisoner. The team infiltrates through rain and mud, discovers a trade log listing fuel, children, and info, then accesses the server room where files reveal EDEN NODE ACTIVE, EDENROT DORMANCY RESPONSE, maritime routes, naval keys, and an immune child bounty. Trung recovers the drive. Mai breaks free from inside during the alarm. Fire from the kiln ignites fuel on the flooded yard, creating the chapter's title image: fire burning under rain. Horde drawn by the explosion forces everyone to retreat. On the canal escape bridge, Hoang is trapped by a steel trap. Trung chooses not to kill or abandon him, saying he will not let Binh learn that pain is a reason to kill. Back at base, Binh asks if Trung killed Hoang; Trung says no, not because he forgives, but because Binh was watching. Doctor decodes the server files, confirming the Architect broker network tracking Edenrot dormancy and maritime logistics. The blackboard rule "Here, children have names" is repaired by Binh. The chapter ends with the understanding that "Home" must be abandoned; the group must prepare to leave for the sea.
