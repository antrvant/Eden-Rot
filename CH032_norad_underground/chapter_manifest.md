## Canon Summary

The Heartland Convoy reaches NORAD's mountain bunker to seize the satellite interface. The bunker has automated defenses, remnant faction under Captain Hayes, limited shelter capacity, and lower levels occupied by a King infected variant. Trung uses Mentor's dog tag and legacy phrase "No one is left behind just because the list is full" to open the first door layer. Mai establishes a public intake ledger and transparent shelter rotation instead of priority lists. When the King infected sends low-frequency pulses to coordinate hordes through bunker speakers, Binh identifies the one-one-two rhythm from a children's clapping game, and Thu reverses the speakers against it. Trung splits teams for the satellite assault but returns to defend the intake tunnel, proving people over system. VALE attempts to inject target prioritization authority during satellite boot but is blocked by air-gap and safe mode. The satellite network partially opens with a global broadcast window, and Architect listener fragments are detected.
