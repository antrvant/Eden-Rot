## Faction Notes

- `FAC_CH015_FAMILY_CORE` is not a traditional faction. It represents the tension between family loyalty and base governance. Trung must give up family-as-authority.
- `FAC_CH015_WORKERS` is the birth of citizenship. Worker Lead's challenge prevents the base from becoming Trung's household plus dependents.
- `FAC_CH015_EDEN_NETWORK` should not be fully revealed. Only their methods are visible: medicine, labels, rumors, fake mothers. The network itself remains mysterious.
- `FAC_CH015_EDEN_SCOUT` (Hanh) can return in later chapters as prisoner, messenger, or casualty depending on player choice.
- `FAC_CH015_CHILDREN` is not a voting faction but must have a voice. Mai's role ensures children are not forgotten or used as political tools.
- Reputation groups unlocked in this chapter: Family Core, Workers, Children, Medical, Security.
