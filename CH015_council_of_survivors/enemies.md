## Enemy Handling Notes

- Chapter 15 has no traditional infected combat. The "enemies" are social, medical, and infiltration threats.
- `ENM_CH015_SOCIAL_PARANOIA` is the true antagonist: fear makes reasonable people cruel.
- `ENM_CH015_EDEN_SCOUT_INFILTRATOR` should not be written as purely evil. Hanh is a victim coerced by EDEN using her sick brother as leverage.
- `ENM_CH015_EDENROT_LANGUAGE` is the most dangerous enemy because it operates even when no EDEN agent is present. Once survivors start calling Binh by his code, the base is infected before anyone is bitten.
- `ENM_CH015_FEVER_OUTBREAK` is resolved in this chapter if medicine is used. If refused, it becomes a continuity thread for Chapter 16.
- `ENM_CH015_NIGHT_PROBE` is referenced in scout intel but not directly played. It foreshadows EDEN's awareness of the base.
