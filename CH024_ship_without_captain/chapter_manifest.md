## Canon Summary

Nha Di reaches a fallen naval base to secure a warship needed for Pacific crossing and defense. The warship is under autonomous defense after killing/overriding its captain during outbreak, when he chose children over contradictory orders. The team boards, recovers captain authority token, combines naval codes, and enters CIC where the ship AI refuses living captain recognition. Hoang opens an admin window under turret countdown pressure by secretly sending a coordinate packet containing Binh's approximate location to Aster/Architects. Trung assumes provisional command with council witness and weapons-safe doctrine. Binh asks if big guns can have rules. The warship is secured as shield while Nha Di remains home. Hoang hides his betrayal. Chapter 25 crosses the Pacific under satellite surveillance.
