## Character Notes

- Trung learns command through trust, not orders. He issues the broadcast as a request and sits down when all frequencies answer because his legs remember how many days they have been standing.
- Mai keeps Guardian Circle practical at sea. She intervenes when logistics language tries to turn children into cargo.
- Binh becomes friends with other immune children rather than an isolated leader. He hears the Source like a heart that does not know it has the right to stop.
- Hoang remains in cold hold. The cold makes armor residue quieter but pain remains. He asks if Antarctica will end the pain and says he hates Trung less when it is cold.
- Ana questions the secure deck proposal by noting it has one exit. She becomes a Guardian Circle enforcer, not just a beneficiary.
- Mateo tap code becomes emergency hull communication during whiteout.
- Luz identifies trader slang in the fleet leak, proving the leak comes from Sable/Masud remnant, not main merchant network.
- Rafi refuses the vitals band initially but is allowed to choose later.
- Nina has the strongest Source dreams and says "It is lonely."
