## Canon Summary

Chapter 48 opens Act 8 with the final alliance assembly. Trung issues a voluntary broadcast sharing the 30-day countdown without exposing child data. One by one, allied frequencies respond: Heartland, NORAD, tribal alliance, merchant network, naval remnants, Europe cells, and Amazon survivors. The fleet crosses the Southern Ocean under Guardian Circle rules, with children placed in mixed family/guardian pods as passengers with rights, not cargo. Hoang remains in cold hold stasis, briefly waking to ask if Antarctica will end his pain. Source dreams affect Binh and the immune children simultaneously, showing a heart under ice and a choice between warm green cure and white reset. A fleet leak is contained through Luz's trader slang identification without collective child search. The team rescues the ice-trapped Saint Lark vessel carrying 32 people. Aurora-like static syncs children's heartbeats as the fleet approaches Antarctica. A whiteout storm cuts central command, forcing the fleet to rely on distributed trust protocols and Mateo's hull tap code. Ships emerge near the Antarctic ice shelf, damaged and scattered, with a black structure pulsing beneath blue ice. Long-range communications die completely. An internal message appears: PROCEED UNDER ICE. Binh tells Trung he hears the heart under the ice.
