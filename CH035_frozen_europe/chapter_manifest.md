## Canon Summary

Rebirth crosses the Atlantic with Captain Moreno's portless fleet, trading NORAD star/navigation data for Channel crossing. The team discovers Europe's cold zones are not natural weather but terraforming atmospheric seeding. Landing on frozen UK coast, they find evacuation zones preserved mid-rescue. They learn the Ice horde reanimates after being shot unless burned, shattered, or brain stem destroyed. A false relief waystation using old humanitarian flags is exposed and its keeper Ingrid freed. The team camps in a service tunnel, builds heat kits, and verifies Teacher Amelie's Paris signal using the child-safety phrase. Amelie warns about something in the metro tunnels imitating absent children's voices. The chapter ends with the team securing the Europe entry route and receiving the Mimic voice hook for Chapter 36.
