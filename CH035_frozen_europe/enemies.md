## Ice Horde Handling Note

The Ice horde is the primary new enemy type. Canonical rules: shooting down does not count as dead. Countermeasures are fire/heat damage, melee shatter of frozen bodies, and precision brain stem shots. Binh's observation of the hand twitch timing is key to survival.
