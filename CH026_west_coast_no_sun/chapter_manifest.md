## Canon Summary

After crossing the Pacific, the group reaches the smoke-covered West Coast of North America. Binh asks where the sun is; Mai says they will bring their own light. The landing party secures an old Coast Guard station, disabling an automated turret that mistakes them for quarantine breach. Mara, a former paramedic leading local survivors, confronts the warship newcomers. Mai leads first contact, lowers weapons, and names the group "Rebirth Alliance" externally. A drone observer shifts from watching the warship to tracking Binh specifically, transmitting IMMUNE VECTOR LANDED and EDENROT SURVEILLANCE LINK ACTIVE. Hoang tries to confess his coordinate packet betrayal but the drone alarm interrupts. Doctor detects signal from Silicon Valley AI core. Mara warns that people who go toward the Valley do not return or return "watched." The drone disappears pointing toward Silicon Valley. Binh writes chalk on an American road barrier. Act 5 begins.
