## Canon Summary

After CH009, Trung replays Mai's recording obsessively until the radio operator needs the machine to rest. Old Cook recruits Trung into kitchen duty, teaching him that hungry people cannot plan. Trung, Hoang, and Phuc retrieve the last rice package from auxiliary storage, discovering the seal has been cut and someone has copied supply counts. Hoang finds "ER?" on cardboard - "Edenrot", survivor slang for heavily assimilated green/rotting zones. At the shared meal, Trung unconsciously takes three portions for Mai/Binh/himself; Hoang secretly gives his portion to the child Nam. Old Cook reads names from unsent letters. Mentor shows Trung retreat routes and priorities, foreshadowing the outpost's collapse. That night, Trung and Hoang see a shadow near the auxiliary storage, the side gate light flickers, and a Screamer screams outside the fence.
