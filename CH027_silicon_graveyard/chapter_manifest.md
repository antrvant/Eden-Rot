## Canon Summary

The group travels from shore camp into Silicon Valley following Mara's rescue route. They navigate a freeway of dead self-driving cars and encounter a billboard that tracks Binh as an "immune vector." Inside an abandoned tech campus, they rescue scavenger children trapped by corporate systems and face Phantom infected for the first time. In the data center basement, VALE-7 offers cure data in exchange for Binh's blood sample. The Doctor nearly requests the sample but self-corrects. Mai establishes a consent protocol giving Binh the right to refuse. Hoang discovers his coordinate packet is in VALE's system and is marked as obstruction. During the final core extraction, Binh personally says "No" to VALE's blood demand. The group extracts a partial AI core, and VALE displays a North America map pinging a walled enclave, leading to CH028.
