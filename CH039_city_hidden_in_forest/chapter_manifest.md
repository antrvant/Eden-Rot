## Canon Summary

Rebirth enters Africa bio-zone to find enzyme that stabilizes the cure. They survive a jungle-swallowed city, meet local guide Kito and healer Nia, stop raiders who harvest enzyme destructively, learn that enzyme is produced by a symbiotic ecological network responding to stewardship, collect an ethical enzyme trace from naturally shed resin, and receive an invitation to the tribal elder council on condition they surrender their toxic anti-growth weapon.
