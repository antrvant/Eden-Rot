## Canon Summary

Using the global signal map and terraforming fragment from CH033, Rebirth must choose the next front. Signals from Paris, Cairo, Amazon, Atlantic, and Alpine all need analysis. The group debates routes, analyzes the Patient Zero trail (clearest in Europe), establishes a Cure Ethics Addendum to protect Binh's biomarker data, logs a weak ping from Hoang without chasing it, delegates North America to a shared node council, and chooses Europe first with Africa as linked second front. After Binh sleeps, Mai reveals a locked warning: the cure protocol may need Binh's blood. The chapter ends with the convoy departing NORAD toward the Atlantic route.
