## Canon Summary

The Rebirth convoy follows Red Canyon radar to rescue kidnapped immune children from the Cult of Evolution, a religious group believing the virus is purification and immune children are "new evolutionary form." Prophet Dao manipulates grieving parents through comfort language and care-as-cage tactics. The team infiltrates as pilgrims, discovers the nursery where Mother Elian keeps children locked while singing to them, and finds ascension masks containing micro-needles and neurotoxin. Hoang confesses to Trung that he sent coordinate packets that helped satellite/VALE track Binh. During the ritual, Binh refuses worship by saying "I'm scared. And I'm still Binh," which breaks the ritual's naming logic. Mother Elian opens the nursery when Noah asks for his name back. Dao releases "Blessed" infected controlled by sound, and Hoang saves Binh from one before leaving the convoy with his packet log and a note: "This time I didn't sell you. Don't ask why."
