## Canon Summary

Bio-storm forces the group onto an unnamed island in the South China Sea. The vessel is damaged and cannot continue without parts from the island's abandoned lighthouse relay and fishing village. While Thu and Hoang repair the boat, Mai rebuilds community routines aboard by establishing a classroom on deck. The team discovers the island was a hidden maritime relay tied to EDEN, classified by Architects as EDENROT CLASS: ISLAND RELAY. They are attacked by drowned infected as the relay wakes. The boat is repaired, named "Nha Di" (Home That Moves) by Binh, and escapes toward the coastal tech port in Chapter 22. Binh learns that "Home" can have a new name without betraying the old one.
